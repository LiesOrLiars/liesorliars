FAQ

What is this archive?

Lies or Liars is a cold documentary archive. Case files come from primary documents. Court records. Police files. Official papers. What the document says is what we report.

How are files sourced?

From the public record. We cite the document. We do not speculate.

Exclusive files. They are not on the site. They are not on X. The form is on the home page.

Arrest the Epstein List is an arcade on this site. It is satire. Named figures come from public flight logs and court releases.
