The Brand Was His Initials

Published 25 October 2026

How this file was sourced: official records only, listed in Sources below.

◆Part 1: The Brand

The women were required to be nude. They were required to say the words. Master, please brand me. It would be an honor, an honor that I want to wear for the rest of my life.

The words are recorded in the 2022 opinion of the United States Court of Appeals for the Second Circuit. The symbol burned into the women was presented as part of a secret society for women. The court recorded what it was. Unbeknownst to them, it consisted of Keith Raniere's initials.

The FBI complaint against Raniere stated that many of the women were branded in their pubic regions with a cauterizing pen in a process that took 20 to 30 minutes. One woman was ordered to film. The others held down the woman being branded. Some were told the lines stood for air, earth and water and the pen stood for sealing with fire. The complaint stated that Raniere later acknowledged to one victim that his initials were in the brand as a form of tribute. Masters then told the women that the videos of the branding and the photographs of their brands were more collateral.

On 19 June 2019 a federal jury in Brooklyn convicted Raniere of all 7 counts against him. On 27 October 2020 Judge Nicholas G. Garaufis sentenced him to 120 years in prison.

◆Part 2: The Company

According to the complaint, in about 1998 Raniere founded Executive Success Programs, a series of workshops designed to actualize human potential. In about 2003 he founded NXIVM as an umbrella organisation. It was headquartered in Albany, New York, and operated centres in the United States, Canada, Central America and Mexico.

The complaint stated that a 5 day workshop could cost up to 5,000 dollars. Members wore coloured sashes that marked their rank. Many fell into debt paying for courses. Members were often required to sign non disclosure agreements.

Before NXIVM, Raniere ran a multilevel marketing scheme called Consumers' Buyline. The complaint stated it was forced to close after a settlement with the New York Attorney General in 1997.

Members called Raniere the Vanguard. According to the complaint, every August they paid 2,000 dollars or more to gather at Silver Bay, New York, for Vanguard Week, held in honour of Raniere, whose birthday is 26 August.

The complaint stated that Raniere kept a rotating group of 15 to 20 women with whom he had sexual relationships. They were not permitted to have sexual relationships with anyone else.

◆Part 3: The Vow

In 2015 Raniere created DOS. The letters stood for Dominus Obsequious Sororium. The Second Circuit translated the phrase as roughly Lord or Master of the Obedient Female Companions.

DOS was a pyramid. Raniere was at the top. Below him were first line masters. Below them were their slaves. Apart from Raniere, every member was a woman. New recruits were told it was a women only secret society. His role was concealed from them.

The masters recruited mostly from inside NXIVM. They targeted women who were experiencing difficulties in their lives.

To join, a recruit had to hand over collateral. The court listed what that meant. Sexually explicit photographs and videos. Rights to financial assets. Letters containing damaging accusations, true or untrue, about family members and friends. The complaint stated that most were not told at the start that they would have to provide more collateral every month.

The complaint described an email sent to Raniere's account on 10 August 2015. The writer pledged her full and complete life to him as her master. Her collateral included a letter that would destroy the character of her parents, a contract handing custody of any children she bore to Raniere if she broke her commitment, a contract transferring ownership of her home if she broke it, and a letter to social services alleging abuse of her nephews.

According to the complaint, on 1 October 2015 Raniere told a woman in a chat that it would be good for her to own a slave for him, one she could groom and use as a tool to pleasure him.

◆Part 4: The Assignments

Slaves performed acts of care for their masters. The court listed groceries, editing videos, cleaning and organising. The complaint stated the understanding was that a master should end up with the work of at least 1 full time employee.

The complaint stated that slaves took part in readiness drills, answering their masters by text at any hour of the day or night. It recorded severe sleep deprivation. Ice cold showers. Standing for an hour at 4am. Diets so strict that every food eaten had to be documented. One woman was kept on 860 to 1000 calories a day. Others were ordered to be celibate.

The complaint stated that one master told her slaves that she could be paddled or put in a cage by her own master if they failed. Her master was Raniere.

The Second Circuit recorded the rest. In some cases masters assigned their slaves to engage in sexual conduct with Raniere and implied that collateral might be released if they refused.

◆Part 5: Nicole

In February 2016 Allison Mack, a DOS master, recruited a woman the court called Nicole.

Nicole provided letters falsely alleging sexual abuse by her father, other damaging accusations, and a sexually explicit video of herself. She was later required to hand over credit card authorizations and the right to her grandmother's wedding ring.

Mack assigned Nicole to tell Raniere that she would do anything that he asked.

On 31 May 2016 Raniere called Mack while Nicole was at Mack's house. Nicole was sent outside to meet him. He blindfolded her, put her in a car and drove her to a house. He led her through some trees and into a building. He told her to undress and tied her to a table. Another person, whose identity Nicole did not know, performed oral sex on her. When Nicole told Mack what had happened, Mack called her really brave.

Mack regularly required Nicole and other slaves to pose for nude photographs. Nicole testified that she understood that breaking her commitment would mean her collateral would be released.

A second woman, Jay, was given a special assignment by Mack. Seduce Raniere and have him take a naked picture. Jay refused. She decided to leave DOS.

◆Part 6: Camila And Daniela

The Justice Department stated that Raniere began having sex with a member identified as Camila when she was 15 years old. He was 45. He took naked photographs of her when she was a minor. The jury found proved 2 acts of sexual exploitation of a child, dated 2 November 2005 and 24 November 2005, and possession of child pornography.

Daniela was a member of his inner circle. The Justice Department stated that Raniere conspired to bring her across the Canadian border into the United States with a fake identification card bearing the name of a dead woman. Daniela testified that she helped Raniere hack into the email accounts of people he regarded as enemies of NXIVM.

The Justice Department stated that Raniere and his co conspirators confined her to a room for nearly 2 years. She was told that if she left the room she would be sent to Mexico without identification documents. She went months without human contact. She was denied prompt medical care. She wrote hundreds of letters to Raniere asking to be released. She testified that while confined she contemplated suicide.

The jury found proved the trafficking of Daniela for labor and services and document servitude.

◆Part 7: The Villa

The complaint stated that in May 2017 a DOS slave who was also a high ranking member of NXIVM defected in a public way. In October 2017 the New York Times published an article revealing the existence of DOS.

According to the complaint, several weeks later, after the FBI began interviewing witnesses, Raniere flew to Mexico with an heiress who sat on NXIVM's Executive Board. Women who had left DOS began receiving cease and desist letters from a Mexican attorney. The complaint stated that emails showed Raniere and the heiress had orchestrated them.

An FBI special agent swore the complaint in February 2018. It was filed under seal. On 26 March 2018 it was unsealed in Brooklyn. The Justice Department stated that Raniere had been found the day before outside Puerto Vallarta, Mexico, in a luxury villa, and deported by Mexican authorities.

Each of his co defendants pleaded guilty. Nancy Salzman, NXIVM's president and co founder, on 12 March 2019. Lauren Salzman, a first line DOS master, on 25 March 2019. Allison Mack on 8 April 2019. Clare Bronfman, a member of NXIVM's Executive Board, and Kathy Russell, its bookkeeper, on 19 April 2019.

◆Part 8: The Record

The trial lasted 6 weeks. Raniere was convicted of racketeering conspiracy, racketeering, forced labor conspiracy, wire fraud conspiracy, sex trafficking conspiracy, sex trafficking of Nicole and attempted sex trafficking of Jay. The jury found every alleged racketeering act proved, including identity theft, sexual exploitation of a child, extortion and forced labor.

At his sentencing on 27 October 2020 the court heard victim impact statements from 15 people. He received 120 years. Clare Bronfman had been sentenced the month before to 81 months.

On 30 June 2021 Allison Mack was sentenced to 36 months in prison and fined 20,000 dollars. Judge Garaufis told her what she had said to the women she recruited. You told them that Keith Raniere was not involved.

Raniere appealed. He argued that a commercial sex act required something of monetary or financial value and that the exploitation had to be for profit. On 9 December 2022 the Second Circuit rejected the argument. It held that the thing of value need not be monetary or financial and that the exploitation need not be for profit. His sex trafficking convictions were affirmed.

The women were told DOS was a women only society. The man at the top was hidden from them. Their secrets, their assets and explicit images of them were taken as collateral. The brand was his initials.
