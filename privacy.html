Privacy

This policy covers https://liesorliars.com.

Contact: liars@liesorliars.com

What data we hold

Newsletter email. If you join the list, EmailOctopus stores your email address. That is how we send files that are not on the site.

Analytics. Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics run only if you accept. If you choose Essential only, those tools do not load.

Hosting logs. The site is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log requests. A log can include IP address, browser, and time.

We do not sell your data. We do not trade it. We do not use it for ads.

Cookies

The site works without analytics. A small record in your browser stores your choice. Accept or Essential only.

To choose again, clear this site data in your browser.

Third parties

EmailOctopus processes newsletter email.

Google processes analytics if you accept.

GitHub processes hosting logs.

Your rights

UK and EU law gives you rights over personal data.

You can ask what we hold.

You can ask us to correct it.

You can ask us to delete it.

You can object to analytics.

You can complain to the ICO in the UK.

You can complain to your local authority in the EU.

Email liars@liesorliars.com to use these rights.

Children

This archive is not aimed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page.
